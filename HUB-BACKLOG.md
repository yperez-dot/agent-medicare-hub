
## 💰 Commission Calculator (added 2026-07-13)
**Source:** Yahoska screenshot
**Placement:** Tools & Perks page (or standalone page)

**What it does:**
- Select Carrier (UHC, Humana, Aetna, Doctors, Solis, HealthSun, Oscar Health)
- Select Product (Medicare Advantage, Med Supp, ACA, Dental)
- Enter Monthly Premium ($)
- Shows: Rate Applied % + Estimated Commission ($)
