How to Use This Library

Three channels, one strategy. Pick the channel that fits your strengths — then commit.

Pick Your Lead Generation Channel(s) You don't have to do all three at once
3 ⌄

Community Events

Best For

Agents who are outgoing, comfortable in groups, and energized by in-person presence. Senior centers, churches, 55+ HOAs.

Provider Offices

Best For

Agents who are patient, professional, and good at building one-on-one relationships. PCPs, cardiologists, podiatrists.

Social Media

Best For

Agents who write well, prefer working solo, or want to build presence without leaving their desk. Scales infinitely.

The 90-Day Build Plan Don't try everything at once
3 ⌄

Month 1: Foundation

Days 1-30

Pick 3 community venues + 3 provider offices to target. Send first outreach. Start posting 3x/week on social. Read all Phase 1 playbooks.

Month 2: Consistency

Days 31-60

Complete first community visits. Drop-by at provider offices every 2 weeks. Follow up with leads. Post consistently. Read Phase 2 playbooks.

Month 3: Momentum

Days 61-90

Propose your first carrier-sponsored in-service. Convert community event leads to enrollments. Lock in monthly venue rhythm. Read Phase 3 playbooks.

The Lead Generation Math What's realistic from each channel
3 ⌄

Community Events

Realistic Year 1

3 venues × monthly visits × full year = 36 touchpoints. Expect 15-25 enrollments from this channel after the first year.

Provider Offices

Highest ROI

4 offices × bi-weekly visits + 1 in-service per office per year = 50-100 enrollments annually once mature (6+ months in).

Social Media

Slowest Build

3 posts/week × 52 weeks = 156 posts/year. Inbound leads start trickling in around month 6, accelerate at month 12+. Long game.
