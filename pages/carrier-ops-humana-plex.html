Humana · AEP 2027

PLEX plan exits

Operational guidance for members affected by upcoming Humana plan exits and non-renewals.

Member expectation: impacted members are not automatically enrolled in another plan, and some may not have an alternative Humana plan.

Agent steps and timing

  • Identify impacted members in Vantage → My Humana Business.
  • Meet with members after October 1 and complete a thorough Needs Analysis for the best-fit Humana option.
  • Non-renewal letters are expected in members’ homes by approximately October 2.
  • The dedicated PLEX support line begins October 12: 866-753-4920.
  • Members may use the support line to enroll in a different Humana plan; the agent remains Agent of Record.
  • Contact your Humana relationship partner with questions.
