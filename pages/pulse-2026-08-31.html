🚨  ACTION · AGENT

Six weeks to AEP 2027 — finish certs before September blackouts

2-minute read

The Annual Enrollment Period for 2027 coverage runs October 15 through December 7, 2026. CMS’s CY2027 marketing and communications rules apply beginning October 1, 2026. Industry calendars show most carrier certification and contracting blackout windows opening September 1 through October 1. 2027 Medicare and FWA training has been live since June 22. CMS also set August 31, 2026 as the CY2027 MA contract-execution deadline, with remaining contracts expected to be fully signed by mid-September. Scope of Appointment still must be completed before any plan-specific marketing conversation.

What this means for you: Close remaining 2027 carrier certifications this week and confirm Ready-to-Sell in every portal you use. Do not market 2027 plans before October 1. Restock SOA, call-recording, and needs-analysis workflows now — do not wait until October 14.

Source: ICRC Key 2026 Medicare Advantage Dates; Federal Register CY2027 MA and Part D final rule, April 6, 2026

📋  IMPORTANT · CMS

CMS says it blocked more than $1.6 billion in suspect Medicare lab payments

2-minute read

On August 28, CMS announced it has prevented more than $1.6 billion in potentially fraudulent Medicare laboratory payments since the start of the current administration. The agency cited $732 million tied to 157 providers revoked from Medicare, more than $500 million stopped through 185 payment suspensions after reviewing about 600 labs, more than $276 million from 442 identified overpayments, and $127 million tied to law-enforcement referrals. CMS said labs billed for tests never performed or not medically necessary, including genetic and pathogen testing. For fiscal year 2026 to date, CMS said it has identified $1.8 billion in Medicare overpayments, collected $378 million, and suspended more than $539 million.

What this means for you: Use this as a counseling point, not a sales pitch. If a client mentions a surprise lab or genetic-test bill, tell them to hang up on unsolicited testing offers and report suspect Medicare activity to 1-800-MEDICARE or Florida SHINE.

Source: CMS / Fierce Healthcare, August 28, 2026

📋  IMPORTANT · LEGAL

The Villages Health agrees to $541.5 million Medicare Advantage coding settlement

2-minute read

The U.S. Department of Justice announced August 26 that The Villages Health System LLC, based in The Villages, Florida, agreed to a $541.5 million False Claims Act settlement. DOJ alleged the provider submitted invalid diagnosis codes from 2020 through 2024 that inflated Medicare Advantage risk-adjusted payments. The U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Middle District of Florida approved the deal on August 25. DOJ said the organization self-disclosed through the HHS-OIG protocol in December 2024, and that the Medicare Advantage organizations that received the codes are returning related overpayments to CMS. DOJ stated the claims resolved are allegations only and there has been no determination of liability.

What this means for you: Central Florida clients will see this in local news. Stay factual: it is a provider coding case, not a plan cancellation. Do not speculate about plan quality. If a client asks, walk the Annual Notice of Change and a full AEP review.

Source: U.S. Department of Justice, August 26, 2026

🚨  ACTION · FLORIDA

Naples health system will drop two Medicare Advantage contracts in 2027

2-minute read

Naples Comprehensive Health told patients on August 26 that beginning January 1, 2027, it will no longer accept Cigna Medicare Advantage plans or Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans. NCH said patients can continue care through Original Medicare, a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy, or any Medicare Advantage plan that remains in-network with NCH. The hospital asked patients to prepare during AEP, October 15–December 7, 2026. This is a local network change, not a CMS enrollment rule.

What this means for you: Southwest Florida agents should inventory clients who use NCH. Confirm 2027 provider directories when they post. Document each client’s doctors and drugs before any plan discussion. Do not steer. Present every option the client is eligible for, including remaining in Original Medicare with a Medigap policy if that matches their needs.

Source: Gulf Coast News / NCH, August 26, 2026

📋  IMPORTANT · POLICY

CMS capacity limits mean some 2027 Medicare Advantage plans may close to new members

2-minute read

Modern Healthcare reported August 28 that Medicare Advantage enrollment caps are expected to reshape 2027. CMS’s CY2027 bid guidance lets MA organizations request an enrollment capacity limit on a plan. CMS will only accept limits at or above a plan’s enrollment as of June 1, 2026, and plans may not disenroll current members to hit a cap. Once a plan is closed, it must stay closed to new enrollments until attrition opens a slot. AEP shopping will not look the same in every Florida county.

What this means for you: Do not assume every 2026 plan will take new 2027 members. Watch carrier 2027 plan lists and closed-to-new-members flags when they drop. Have a backup comparison ready. Capacity closures are first-come during AEP — October 15 is not the same as late November.

Source: Modern Healthcare, August 28, 2026; CMS CY2027 Part C Bid Review Memorandum, April 22, 2026

📰  FYI · CMS

CMS ends the six-month freeze on new DMEPOS supplier enrollments

2-minute read

CMS announced August 27 that its six-month moratorium on initial Medicare enrollment for seven categories of durable medical equipment, prosthetics, orthotics, and supplies (DMEPOS) suppliers has expired. National provider enrollment contractors are again accepting applications. CMS urged suppliers to file promptly so applications can be processed before the bidding window for the 2028 DMEPOS Competitive Bidding Program. Separately, on August 31 MedTech Dive reported CMS finalized a rule to pay for certain continuous glucose monitors, insulin pumps, and diabetes supplies on a bundled monthly rental under competitive bidding, with bidding later in 2027.

What this means for you: Clients on Original Medicare who use CGMs, pumps, or mail-order diabetes supplies may see supplier and payment changes in coming years. For 2027 AEP conversations, confirm the client’s current DME supplier is still billing Medicare. Do not promise device brands.

Source: AHA News / CMS, August 28, 2026; MedTech Dive, August 31, 2026
