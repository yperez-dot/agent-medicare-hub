— THE WEEK IN MEDICARE —

📋  IMPORTANT · CMS

CMS updates 2027 enrollment guidance before AEP

2-minute read

CMS updated its 2027 Medicare Advantage and Part D enrollment and disenrollment guidance on September 15, 2026. The guidance adds operational details, including the required availability of paper enrollment forms, and applies to 2027 requests beginning January 1, 2027; plans may optionally use the updated guidance for AEP enrollments effective January 1. Medicare Open Enrollment remains October 15 through December 7. ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/enrollment-renewal/managed-care-eligibility-enrollment?utm_source=openai))

What this means for you: Review each carrier’s current enrollment workflow before AEP. Confirm that your submission process, election-period documentation, paper-form process, and effective-date handling match the latest carrier instructions.

Source: CMS Medicare Managed Care Eligibility and Enrollment / CY 2027 Enrollment and Disenrollment Guidance — September 15, 2026 ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/enrollment-renewal/managed-care-eligibility-enrollment?utm_source=openai))

🚨  ACTION · AEPCERTS

2027 agent and broker training guidelines are posted

2-minute read

CMS has posted the CY 2027 Agent/Broker Training and Testing Guidelines. The materials reinforce core election-period rules, including that the MA OEP is not an opportunity for someone in Original Medicare to newly join an MA plan. AEP is October 15–December 7, and the MA OEP is January 1–March 31 for eligible individuals already enrolled in Medicare Advantage. ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-drug-plans/managed-care-marketing/models-standard-documents-educational-materials?utm_source=openai))

What this means for you: Finish any remaining carrier certifications and keep the CMS training materials available for quick reference. Be especially precise when explaining AEP, MA OEP, and enrollment effective dates.

Source: CMS Agent/Broker Training & Testing Guidelines and Medicare marketing materials — September 2026 ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-drug-plans/managed-care-marketing/models-standard-documents-educational-materials?utm_source=openai))

📰  FYI · POLICY

Part D’s 2027 national premium benchmarks are set

2-minute read

CMS says the 2027 national average monthly bid amount is $296.05 and the 2027 Part D national base beneficiary premium is $41.33. CMS is also ending the voluntary Part D Premium Stabilization Demonstration after 2026, returning standalone prescription drug plans to traditional market conditions for 2027. These are national benchmarks, not plan-specific premiums. ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicare-part-d-2027-national-average-monthly-bid-amount-information?utm_source=openai))

What this means for you: Use the upcoming plan materials and formularies—not national benchmarks alone—when reviewing a beneficiary’s drug coverage. Recheck premiums, tiers, utilization rules, pharmacies, and annual cost exposure during every review.

Source: CMS, Medicare Part D 2027 National Average Monthly Bid Amount Information — July 28, 2026 ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicare-part-d-2027-national-average-monthly-bid-amount-information?utm_source=openai))

📋  IMPORTANT · FLORIDA

Florida’s CMS Plan transition begins October 1

2-minute read

Florida AHCA reports that Molina Healthcare of Florida will operate the statewide CMS Plan beginning October 1, 2026, replacing Sunshine State Health Plan as administrator. AHCA says current members will transition automatically, while existing appointments, prescriptions, and prior authorizations are expected to be honored for continuity of care. ([ahca.myflorida.com](https://ahca.myflorida.com/medicaid/statewide-medicaid-managed-care/2025-2030-smmc-plans/cms-plan-transition.html?utm_source=openai))

What this means for you: For dual-eligible households connected to the Florida CMS Plan, verify the member’s new plan and provider details before discussing 2027 coverage. Do not assume that a familiar plan name, provider, pharmacy, or authorization will carry over without checking current records.

Source: Florida Agency for Health Care Administration, CMS Plan Transition — September 2026 ([ahca.myflorida.com](https://ahca.myflorida.com/medicaid/statewide-medicaid-managed-care/2025-2030-smmc-plans/cms-plan-transition.html?utm_source=openai))

🚨  ACTION · CMS

D-SNP alignment rules matter more for 2027 conversations

2-minute read

CMS says provisions taking effect in 2027 will limit enrollment in certain D-SNPs to individuals also enrolled in an affiliated Medicaid managed care organization. The rules also limit how many D-SNP plan benefit packages related organizations can offer in the same service area as an affiliated Medicaid MCO. Medicaid status and plan alignment are central eligibility checks—not afterthoughts. ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/medicaid-coordination/about/dsnps?utm_source=openai))

What this means for you: For dual-eligible clients, confirm current Medicaid enrollment, service-area eligibility, and plan alignment before presenting enrollment options. Keep Medicare and Medicaid facts separate and document what was verified.

Source: CMS, D-SNPs: Integration & Unified Appeals and Grievance Requirements — updated August 11, 2026 ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/medicaid-coordination/about/dsnps?utm_source=openai))
