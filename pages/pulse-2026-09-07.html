— THE WEEK IN MEDICARE —

🚨  ACTION · CMS

2027 landscape is due this month; AEP opens October 15

2-minute read

CMS said it will release final average Medicare Advantage and Part D premiums, plus the full 2027 landscape, in mid-to-late September. The Annual Election Period for 2027 coverage runs October 15 through December 7, 2026. Medicare Plan Finder typically updates with 2027 options around October 1. Annual Notices of Change should be reaching current enrollees this month. 2027 Star Ratings are expected on Medicare.gov in early to mid-October. Do not take 2027 applications before October 15.

What this means for you: Finish remaining 2027 certifications this week. Review ANOCs after clients receive them. Compare networks, drugs, and premiums only after CMS posts the landscape. Block October 15 on the calendar.

Source: CMS Part D 2027 NAMBA fact sheet, July 28, 2026

📋  IMPORTANT · POLICY

Part D 2027: premium demo ends; OOP cap moves to $2,400

2-minute read

CMS set the 2027 Part D national average monthly bid amount at $296.05, about 24% higher year over year, and will end the Part D Premium Stabilization Demonstration after 2026. The national base beneficiary premium rises to $41.33 from $38.99, still limited by the IRA 6% annual growth cap through 2029. The 2027 Part D out-of-pocket threshold is $2,400, with a $700 standard deductible. CMS has said premiums will rise by less than $10 for most people, with many seeing lower premiums. Plan-level numbers land with the September landscape.

What this means for you: Expect more PDP comparison questions. Walk clients through TrOOP, the $2,400 cap, and Extra Help. Do not quote 2027 premiums until CMS and carriers publish them. Premiums are not the whole cost.

Source: CMS, July 28, 2026; Managed Healthcare Executive, July 30, 2026

📋  IMPORTANT · CMS

2027 Star Ratings reset; CMS eased some agent conversation rules

2-minute read

In the Contract Year 2027 MA and Part D final rule, CMS is not implementing the Excellent Health Outcomes for All reward for 2027 Star Ratings and will keep the historical reward factor. CMS is removing 11 measures that were administrative or showed little plan-to-plan difference, and adding a Part C Depression Screening and Follow-Up measure starting with measurement year 2027 (2029 ratings). The rule also removes restrictions on the time and manner of conversations between beneficiaries and licensed agents, rescinds the mid-year unused supplemental-benefit notice, and tightens SSBCI debit-card rules (real-time eligibility checks, plan-year limited). IRA Part D redesign is codified for 2027 and beyond: no coverage gap, an annual OOP threshold, and $0 enrollee cost sharing in catastrophic.

What this means for you: Refresh how you explain Stars — fewer process measures, more clinical and experience measures. Follow current CMS marketing rules, SOAs, and recording requirements. The conversation-timing change is not a green light for unsolicited contact.

Source: CMS CY 2027 MA and Part D Final Rule fact sheet, April 2, 2026

📰  FYI · FLORIDA

Florida SHINE Medicare classes start this week

2-minute read

Florida SHINE (Serving Health Insurance Needs of Elders) is running virtual Medicare classes starting Monday, September 7, 2026, covering Medicare Basics, Part C, Part D, preventive services, and fraud prevention through late September. SHINE counseling remains unbiased and confidential through the Florida Department of Elder Affairs and local Area Agencies on Aging. Helpline: 1-800-963-5337. Clients will hear a lot of public education between now and AEP.

What this means for you: Point clients to SHINE when they want a second opinion. Keep your presentations compliant and clearly separate from SHINE events. Do not imply affiliation with SHINE or DFS.

Source: Florida SHINE event listings, September 2026

📋  IMPORTANT · LEGAL

Florida provider group settles MA coding case for $541.5 million

2-minute read

DOJ announced on August 26, 2026 that The Villages Health System LLC, headquartered in The Villages, Florida, agreed to a $541.5 million False Claims Act settlement over self-disclosed invalid diagnosis codes submitted to Medicare Advantage organizations from 2020 through 2024. Those plans have agreements with CMS to return related funds. The bankruptcy court approved the settlement on August 25. Separately, CMS said on August 28 it has blocked or recovered more than $1.6 billion in potentially improper Medicare laboratory payments and revoked 157 lab providers.

What this means for you: If clients mention chart reviews, in-home assessments, or lab or DME solicitations, keep the conversation educational. Do not coach diagnoses. Report suspected fraud to 1-800-MEDICARE or Florida SHINE.

Source: U.S. Department of Justice, August 26, 2026; CMS Newsroom, August 28, 2026

🚨  ACTION · AGENT

Five weeks to AEP — lock certs, SOA, and 2027 product training

2-minute read

AHIP 2027 training went live June 22, 2026. Carrier 2027 benefits trainings are opening this month. You still need a compliant Scope of Appointment before a marketing appointment, and you cannot submit 2027 enrollments before October 15. KFF reports 55% of eligible Medicare beneficiaries are in MA in 2026 (35.2 million of 64.2 million). Medicare will spend at least $13.4 billion on MA quality bonuses in 2026, with 68% of MA enrollees in bonus-qualifying plans — the lowest share since 2018.

What this means for you: Audit ready-to-sell status this week. Calendar remaining 2027 product trainings. Confirm SOA and TPMO disclaimer workflows before October. Practice ANOC reviews now so October is execution, not scramble.

Source: KFF, June 5, 2026 and August 12, 2026; Action Benefits AHIP update, May 8, 2026
