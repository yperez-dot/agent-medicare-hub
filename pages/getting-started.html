Before You Get Started

Estimated time to complete: 20–30 minutes

Required:

  • Active state license
  • NPN number
  • AHIP (if applicable for carrier submissions)

Have ready:

  • Driver's license
  • W-9 and direct deposit info (if requested)
  • Proof of address (if requested)

Important: Watch your email for invitations to register for AgentSync and Nextere. Complete both registrations as soon as you receive them.

Common Reasons Contracting Gets Delayed

  • Name mismatch (license vs W-9)
  • Incorrect NPN entered
  • Expired/inactive state license
  • Upload file type issues
  • Missing residency/address documentation

Your First 7 Days

Goal: Try to complete Getting Started + Days 1–2 in one sitting.

Getting Started
  • Look out for email invitations for AgentSync and Nextere
  • Confirm logins work
  • Verify license status + NPN
  • Gather required documents
Days 1–2
  • Complete setup in AgentSync / Nextere
  • Upload required documents
  • Use the guides if anything is unclear
Days 3–4
  • Submit contracting
  • Upload any missing documents
  • Common stall point: name/NPN mismatch or missing PDFs
Days 5–6
  • Check carrier statuses
  • Follow up on any "Action Needed" items
Day 7
  • Confirm submissions and next steps
  • Verify "Ready To Sell" status
  • Prepare to enroll clients once approved
Ready to Sell Checklist
  • Carrier appointment approved
  • AHIP complete (if required)
  • Access to enrollment tools confirmed
