How to Use This Page

The 4-step workflow when you suspect a client may qualify for Medicaid, MSP, or LTC.

The 4-Step Dual-Eligible Workflow From identifying a candidate to enrolling them in the right plan
4 ⌄

1. Screen the Client

Step 1

Use the LTC Screener and/or MSP Screener during your conversation. They take just a few minutes and identify if the client is likely to qualify.

2. Refer to Golden Years

Step 2

Submit the Typeform referral or have the client call 305-832-1104. Golden Years handles the actual application process.

3. Verify Status

Step 3

Once the client is approved, verify their Medicaid level (QMB, SLMB, FBDE, etc.). The level determines which D-SNP plans they qualify for.

4. Enroll in Right Plan

Step 4

Use the D-SNP Landscape doc to find the right plan for the client's level and county. Apply Dual SEP rules for timing.

Common Scenarios Quick lookups for the situations that come up most
4 ⌄

Client has Medicare AND Medicaid

Verify their level (QMB+, FBDE, SLMB+). Most likely candidate for an aligned D-SNP. Use the D-SNP Landscape to find local options.

Client qualifies but isn't enrolled

Use the LTC or MSP screener to confirm likely eligibility. Refer to Golden Years for application help. Don't enroll them in a D-SNP yet — wait for Medicaid approval.

Client losing Medicaid (redetermination)

If a client loses Medicaid, they qualify for a SEP to switch to a non-D-SNP plan. Refer to Golden Years to attempt to restore coverage if appropriate.

Client may qualify for Extra Help / LIS

Apply through SSA directly (online or 1-800-772-1213). LIS approval can dramatically reduce drug costs. Always check this for low-income clients.

Why This Matters for Your Book The strategic case for screening every prospect
3 ⌄

Better Benefits for Clients

Client Win

Duals on the right plan get OTC cards, transportation, dental, vision, and grocery allowances most other Medicare clients don't.

Higher Retention

Agent Win

D-SNP clients tend to stay longer because the benefits feel meaningfully better. Better retention = stronger renewal income.

Referrals Compound

Compounds

Duals tend to be in tight community networks. One happy dual client often refers 2-3 others. The trust effect is real.
