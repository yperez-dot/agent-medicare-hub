HRA incentive

The payment rule changed for applications written on or after March 1, 2026.

C-SNPD-SNP5 calendar days
$50 incentive: available only for an HRA on a Devoted SNP plan—C-SNP or D-SNP—when the HRA is completed within 5 calendar days of enrollment submission.
  • Applies to applications written March 1, 2026 or later.
  • Devoted no longer pays $50 for non-SNP HRAs.
  • Continue to encourage HRAs for every Devoted member; non-SNP HRAs remain important even though they are unpaid.

Source: Devoted broker HRA FAQ and partner notices; CareValue, Fidelis, and HealthSherpa notices align on the effective date, SNP-only payment, and completion window.
