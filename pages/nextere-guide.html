Nextere

Nextere is used for certain carrier contracting processes. Follow the steps below before submitting applications.

When Should I Use Nextere?

Nextere is only used for specific carriers.
It is not required for all contracting processes.
  • Doctors Health
  • Solis Health Plans
  • HealthSun
If you are contracting with other carriers, use AgentSync or the appropriate platform.

Quick Actions

Most agents only need the two actions below.

Watch Tutorial Download Guide (PPT)
Reminder: Transfers often require a release letter. Have it ready to upload when prompted.

Before Submitting (Checklist)

  • Have your license number and NPN ready.
  • Upload your active E&O policy (not expired).
  • If transferring, upload the release letter (carrier requirement).
  • Monitor your email — approvals and requests are time-sensitive.

What Happens Next?

  • Carriers typically review within 3–10 business days.
  • You will receive approval emails directly from the carriers (check spam).
  • Transfers may take longer depending on release requirements.

Common Issues

  • Expired E&O policy
  • Transfer submitted without release letter
  • Carrier emails going to spam
  • Not allowing enough time for review
Tip: If you're waiting for an update, allow up to 10 business days before requesting a status update.

Still Need Help?

  • Active E&O uploaded
  • Release letter uploaded (if transfer)
  • Waited 3–10 business days
  • Checked spam folder for carrier emails
