Medicare 101 (Quick Overview)

Part A (Hospital)

Inpatient hospital, skilled nursing, hospice. Often $0 premium if 40 quarters.

Part B (Medical)

Doctors, outpatient care, preventive services. Monthly premium applies.

Part C (Medicare Advantage)

Private plan alternative that replaces A & B and may bundle drug + extras.

Part D (Prescription Drugs)

Standalone drug plan or included in many Medicare Advantage plans.

Eligibility (Most Common)

  • Turning 65 (main pathway)
  • Disability (SSDI eligibility after a qualifying period)
  • ESRD / ALS (special rules may apply)

Client Talk Track (Simple)

  • "Medicare has parts. A & B are Original Medicare."
  • "Then you either add a Supplement + drug plan, or choose Medicare Advantage."
  • "The right option depends on budget, doctors, meds, and timing."
Agent tip: Always anchor the appointment on the client's effective date and which election period applies — everything else flows from that.
