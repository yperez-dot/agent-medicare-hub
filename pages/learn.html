💊 Negotiated Drugs💊 Medicamentos negociados

Medicare negotiates prices directly with drug companies for some of the highest-cost brand drugs. Negotiated prices (CMS calls them “maximum fair prices”) apply to Medicare Part D — and starting in 2028, Part B. What your client pays still depends on their plan’s copay or coinsurance.

Heads up: the widely quoted “64 drugs” list is a different program — Part B coinsurance reductions under the Medicare Inflation Rebate Program (Jan–Mar 2025 quarter) — not price negotiation.

Medicare negocia los precios directamente con las farmacéuticas para algunos de los medicamentos de marca más costosos. Los precios negociados (CMS los llama “precios justos máximos”) aplican a la Parte D de Medicare — y a partir de 2028, también a la Parte B. Lo que paga el cliente sigue dependiendo del copago o coseguro de su plan.
