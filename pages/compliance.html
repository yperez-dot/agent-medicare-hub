NEW — SOA Rule Change · Effective October 1, 2026

CY2027 Final Rule

The mandatory 48-hour waiting period between obtaining a Scope of Appointment (SOA) and conducting a personal marketing appointment has been eliminated.

Beginning October 1, 2026 for Plan Year 2027 enrollments, appointments may occur the same day an SOA is completed. All other SOA requirements remain in effect.

What Changed

48-hour waiting period eliminated. Same-day appointments permitted starting Oct 1, 2026 for PY27 enrollments.

What Stayed the Same

SOA still required before every personal marketing appointment. New SOA needed when product scope changes.

Electronic Signatures

CMS explicitly confirms electronic signatures and audio/video recordings satisfy the written requirement.

SOA Validity

SOAs remain valid for 12 months. If product scope does not change, a new SOA is not required solely because the plan year changes.

Agent Action Items
  • Obtain and document an SOA before every personal marketing appointment
  • Only discuss products identified within the documented SOA
  • Obtain a new SOA before expanding discussions to additional product lines
  • Retain SOA documentation per CMS record retention requirements
Source

CMS CY2027 Final Rule — 42 CFR §§ 422.2264(c)(3) and 423.2264(c)(3). Bulletin issued via YourFMO. For licensed agent use only — do not distribute to clients.
