Medicaid Levels & Extra Help

In-hub quick reference for LIS copays and Medicare Savings Program levels — classify members without opening a Google Doc.

LIS Extra Help

Covers part or all of monthly premiums, deductibles, and copayments for Medicare drug plans. Members pay copays based on their LIS level — those copays are the operative agent need on a sales call.

  • Auto-enrollment: Medicaid, SSI, or any Medicare Savings Program (QMB / SLMB / QI) automatically enrolls the member in Extra Help.
  • Apply: SSA online or call 1-800-772-1213.
  • Since 2024: full Extra Help benefits only — the old partial LIS level is gone.
2026 Extra Help income / assets (Florida, aligned with the live THEI / HealthExps MSP figures): Individual ≤ $1,903/mo · Couple ≤ $2,575/mo · Assets $17,220. Home and one car are typically excluded. Countable income is often lower than gross (first $20 of most income and first $65 of earned income do not count).

Medicaid Levels / Medicare Savings Program

Use this to classify the member on a call. Benefit distinctions are from the THEI Medicaid & LIS cheat sheet (updated 04/07/2026).

Level Full Medicaid? Pays Part A premium Pays Part B premium Pays deductibles / copays for Medicare services Notes
QMB+ Yes Yes Yes Yes Medicaid coverage of Medicare cost-share and full Medicaid. Member pays nothing except Part D copays.
QMB No Yes Yes Yes Cost-share only — not full Medicaid. Pays Part A & B premiums, deductibles, coinsurance, and copays for Medicare-covered services. Member pays nothing except Part D copays.
QI No No Yes No Part B premium only. State does not pay cost-share. No full Medicaid. Limited slots; must re-apply every year.
SLMB+ Yes No Yes Limited Part B premium + full Medicaid. Generally 0% cost-share when the service is covered by both Medicare and Medicaid. Member may pay when Medicaid does not cover the service.
SLMB No No Yes No Part B premium only. State does not pay cost-share. No full Medicaid.
FBDE Yes No No Limited Full Medicaid. Medicaid may give limited help with Medicare cost-sharing. Generally 0% when both Medicare and Medicaid cover the service; member may pay when Medicaid does not.
