Quick Reference — What Agents Need to Know

Open Enrollment Period

6 months from Part B effective date. Guaranteed issue — no health questions. Best time to sell.

Plan G Select vs Plan G

Same benefits, lower premium. Requires network hospital use for non-emergency care.

GI Events — 63-Day Rule

Loss of creditable coverage, MA trial right. App received within 63 days of termination. Include docs.

Underwriting Knockouts (§6)

YES = ineligible. Cancer, cardiac, COPD, ALS, Alzheimer's, transplants. Review before submitting.

Part B Deductible 2026

$257/year. Plan G does NOT cover it. Plan F does — but only for pre-2020 Medicare eligibility.

AARP Membership — UHC Plans Only

UHC/AARP plans only — not required for other carriers. We work with all major Medigap carriers (Aetna, Cigna, Mutual of Omaha, etc.) — no AARP membership needed for those. For UHC: $20/year, enroll at aarp.org/savetoday or 1-866-331-1964. Agents cannot pay dues for clients. Client can cancel AARP membership after the policy is issued — coverage continues as long as premiums are paid.

Discounts

3% multi-insured + $2/mo EFT OR $24/yr annual pay. EFT and annual pay cannot be combined.

Rate Areas — Florida

Area 1 = South FL (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach). Area 2 = rest of FL. ~$55–60/mo difference on Plan G.

Submitting Applications

JarvisEnroll preferred. Paper mail to PO Box 105331, Atlanta GA 30348. Fax: 1-888-836-3985. No email.

Producer Help Desk

1-888-381-8581 (Option 2 = Español). Also available via Chat on Jarvis.

Member Services (clients)

1-800-523-5800. Verify current hospital network status before any Plan G Select enrollment.

Plans C & F — Restricted

Only for clients eligible for Medicare before Jan 1, 2020. Everyone else → push to Plan G.
